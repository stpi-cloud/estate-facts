# STPi

**Recruitment automation and applicant tracking** · Coldcode SI Labs, Harare, Zimbabwe

> STPi hunts 80 job board families across 21 African markets every day, writes a cover letter and a tailored CV for every match, and sends only after a human taps approve.

STPi is an AI job-search and placement system for recruiters, career coaches and boutique agencies in Africa: it finds the vacancies, drafts the application in the advert's own language, and sends from the candidate's own mailbox once a person approves it.

- Live: https://stpi.whop.site

## Who it is for

Recruiters, career coaches and boutique placement agencies working in South Africa, Zimbabwe and Zambia. Markets: South Africa, Zimbabwe, Zambia, 21 African markets in total.

## What it does

### Hunt

80 job board families and 121 endpoints, including unindexed social posts and newspaper PDFs, across 21 African markets.

### Draft

A relevance score, a cover letter and a tailored CV per match, built only from the profile the recruiter approved.

### Approve

Cards arrive as 'Accountant at Acme, match 87%' and nothing sends without a human tap; drafts expire in 72 hours.

### Send

Print-ready PDFs attached, signed as the candidate, capped per candidate and per day.

### Follow up

Interview invites, questions, rejections and bounced addresses are reported back.

### Employer side

Persona cards, rubric-graded requirements backed by CV quotes, side-by-side compare and OmveoAgent shortlist briefs.

## What you get

- Vacancies found daily across 80 board families and 121 endpoints
- A tailored cover letter and CV per match, never invented facts
- One approval tap per send, from the candidate's own mailbox
- Replies routed back to the recruiter and the candidate

## Questions

**What is STPi?**

STPi is a recruitment automation system that hunts job boards across 21 African markets, drafts a tailored cover letter and CV for each match, and sends only after a human taps approve.

**How much does STPi cost?**

The Done-For-You seat is $49 a month (R850 in South Africa). Studio is $613 a month, Solo is $306 a month and Sovereign is $920 a month for agencies running their own infrastructure. The CV Setup Pack is a one-off $19 (R350), and the demo is free forever at five deliveries a month.

**Is there a free trial?**

Yes. The demo runs the full hunt, draft and PDF pipeline with one active candidate and five deliveries per UTC month, free forever, with no sign-up form and no card.

**Which job boards does STPi search?**

80 job board families over 121 endpoints, including the employers' own ATS boards (Lever, Greenhouse, Ashby, Recruitee, Workable and Personio) alongside PNet, CareerJunction, Careers24, Bizcommunity, iHarareJobs, VacancyMail, JobsZimbabwe, GoZambiaJobs, UNjobs and Impactpool, plus unindexed social posts and newspaper PDFs.

**Does STPi apply to jobs automatically?**

No. Every application waits for a human tap on an approval card; there is no automatic mode, and drafts delete themselves after 72 hours.

**Will automated applications get a candidate's email flagged?**

STPi sends from the candidate's own mailbox with per-candidate and per-day caps, and only after approval, which is what keeps a normal sending pattern.

**How does STPi help an employer facing hundreds of applicants?**

Every requirement in the advert becomes a rubric line graded against a quote from the CV, applicants appear as persona cards, and OmveoAgent prepares an interview shortlist with the evidence behind each name. The hiring manager still makes the call.

**Where does STPi run, and who holds the data?**

STPi is installable software: pip install the wheel and run it on your own machine or server. Nothing about a candidate is held by STPi.
